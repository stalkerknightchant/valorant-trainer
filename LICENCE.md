## 🧩 About

**Valorant Trainer** is a comprehensive training menu for Valorant featuring aim trainer, recoil control practice, crosshair overlay, sensitivity converter, warmup drills, and spray pattern viewer.

Based on popular training tools like **Aim Lab**, **KovaaK's**, and **Aim Trainer Pro**.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🎮 Core Training
- Aim Trainer — flick and tracking drills on configurable targets
- Recoil Control — practice spray patterns for every weapon
- Crosshair Overlay — custom crosshair shapes, colors, and outlines
- Sensitivity Converter — convert sens between games and DPI values
- Warmup Drills — timed routines before ranked matches

### 🎨 Visual & Overlay
- Spray Pattern Viewer — visualize recoil paths per weapon
- FPS Counter Overlay — monitor frame rate in real time
- Hit Marker Sound — audio feedback on target hits

### 🏋️ Practice & Analysis
- Aim Stats — accuracy, reaction time, headshot percentage
- Session History — track progress across training sessions
- Custom Scenarios — build your own drill presets
- Sensitivity Profiles — save and switch between configs
